Return & Refund Policy

Last updated: March 2025

1. Return Eligibility

You may return unopened, unused products within 30 days of delivery for a full refund. Products must be in original packaging.

2. How to Return

Contact us at returns@princepaw.com with your order number. We will provide a return label and instructions. You are responsible for return shipping unless the item is defective.

3. Refund Process

Once we receive your return, we will inspect the product and process your refund within 5-7 business days. Refunds are issued to the original payment method.

4. Non-Refundable Items

Opened or used products cannot be returned for hygiene reasons. Sale items may have different return terms.

5. Damaged or Defective Items

If you receive a damaged or defective product, contact us immediately. We will arrange a replacement or full refund at no extra cost.
