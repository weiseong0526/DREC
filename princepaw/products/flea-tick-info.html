Flea & Tick Defense

Flea & Tick Control

Ingredients

Aqua (Water), Pyrethrins (Natural), Neem Oil, Cedarwood Oil, Peppermint Oil, Eucalyptus Oil, Sodium Lauroyl Sarcosinate, Cocamidopropyl Betaine, Glycerin, Aloe Vera, Citric Acid, Sodium Benzoate.

Product Information

  • Volume: 500ml
  • Suitable for: Dogs & Cats (12 weeks+)
  • pH Level: 6.5–7.0
  • Active: Natural pyrethrins, essential oils
  • Free from: Harsh chemicals, artificial fragrances

How to Use

Wet coat thoroughly. Apply and massage into a lather, ensuring coverage especially around neck and base of tail. Leave on for 5 minutes. Rinse well. Use every 2–4 weeks for prevention, or as needed for active infestations.
