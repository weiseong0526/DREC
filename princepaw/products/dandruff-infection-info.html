Medicated Relief Shampoo

Dandruff / Infection

Ingredients

Aqua (Water), Ketoconazole, Chlorhexidine Gluconate, Salicylic Acid, Colloidal Oatmeal, Tea Tree Oil, Aloe Vera, Glycerin, Cocamidopropyl Betaine, Sodium Lauroyl Sarcosinate, Citric Acid, Sodium Benzoate.

Product Information

  • Volume: 500ml
  • Suitable for: Dogs & Cats with dandruff or mild skin infections
  • pH Level: 5.5–6.5
  • Active ingredients: Ketoconazole (antifungal), Chlorhexidine (antibacterial)
  • Consult vet for: Severe or persistent conditions

How to Use

Wet coat. Apply and massage into a lather. Leave on for 5–10 minutes to allow active ingredients to work. Rinse thoroughly. Use 2–3 times per week as directed by your veterinarian.
