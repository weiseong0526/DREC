Soothing Oatmeal Shampoo

Sensitive / Itchy Skin

Ingredients

Aqua (Water), Colloidal Oatmeal, Aloe Vera Gel, Chamomile Extract, Calendula Extract, Glycerin, Cocamidopropyl Betaine, Sodium Lauroyl Sarcosinate, Allantoin, Panthenol, Vitamin E, Citric Acid, Sodium Benzoate. Free from fragrances, dyes, and sulfates.

Product Information

  • Volume: 500ml
  • Suitable for: Dogs & Cats with sensitive skin
  • pH Level: 6.0–6.5 (hypoallergenic)
  • Fragrance: Unscented
  • Free from: Parabens, sulfates, fragrances, dyes

How to Use

Wet coat with lukewarm water. Apply and massage gently. Leave on for 5 minutes to allow oatmeal to soothe. Rinse thoroughly. Use as needed, up to 2–3 times per week for itchy pets.
