Delivery Policy

Last updated: March 2025

1. Shipping Methods

We offer standard and express shipping. Delivery times vary by location. Standard delivery typically takes 5-7 business days; express takes 2-3 business days.

2. Free Shipping

Free standard shipping on orders over $50. Orders below $50 incur a flat shipping fee of $5.99.

3. Order Processing

Orders are processed within 1-2 business days. You will receive a confirmation email with tracking information once your order ships.

4. Delivery Areas

We currently ship within the United States. International shipping may be available—contact us for details.

5. Tracking

Track your order via the link in your shipping confirmation email or through your account's My Orders page.
