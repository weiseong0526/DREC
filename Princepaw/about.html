It started with one itchy belly.

从一只痒痒的肚皮开始。

In 2021, our co-founder Lina spent three months chasing relief for her rescue dog Marlow's hot spots. Every shampoo seemed to make things worse. Her vet — Dr. Chen — sketched a formula on a napkin. Marlow's belly cleared in nine days. The napkin became Prince Paw.

2021 年，联合创始人 Lina 为她救助的狗狗 Marlow 治疗皮肤热点，整整跑了三个月。她的兽医陈医师在餐巾纸上勾勒出一个配方 — 九天后，Marlow 的肚皮恢复柔嫩。那张餐巾纸，就是 Prince Paw 的开始。

Today, every formula still passes the napkin test: would a vet write this down for their own pet? If not, we don't bottle it.

今天，每一款配方依旧需要通过「餐巾纸测试」：兽医会为自己的爱宠写下这个方子吗？如果不会，我们就不灌瓶。
